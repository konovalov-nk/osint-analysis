House of El: The 4K Is a Lie

We downloaded the 720p, 1080p, 1440p and 2160p renditions of a House of El: AI video, measured the spectrum, round-tripped every frame through 1080p, and compared it to native 4K references. The 4K is an upscale. The person behind it is real. The “am I AI?” panic is a business model.

01

Spectral fingerprint

Radial FFT of the luma plane. Native 4K keeps declining past 0.25 cycles/px; an upscale hits a wall and becomes a noise floor.

02

1080p round-trip

Downscale the 4K frame to 1080p and back. If it reconstructs at 50+ dB, the 4K had no information above 1080p.

03

Native calibration

Encode two native 4K references at the same 6 Mbps with VP9 and run the identical test. Native detail loses much more.
