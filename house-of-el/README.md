# The 4K Is a Lie — House of El forensic report

Open **`index.html`** in a browser. All figures are in **`assets/`**; the full-resolution native 4K reference frames are in **`refs/`**.

## TL;DR

- **The 4K is an upscale.** The 2160p VP9 stream is a 4K container with roughly 1080p information. The talking head is effectively **720–1080p**; the b-roll tops out around **1440p**; no scene shows convincing native 4K detail.
- **The face is the proof.** 4K → 1080p → 4K round-trip: **52.6–54.3 dB**, SSIM **0.986–0.997**. Native 4K references compressed at the same 6 Mbps score **26.6–33.8 dB**. The 4K face's spectrum is a flat noise floor above **0.25 cycles/px**.
- **720p ≈ 1080p for the face.** Upscaling 720p to 4K predicts the actual 4K at **42.01 dB**; 1080p gives **42.07 dB**. A 0.06 dB difference. The 4K adds nothing above 720p for the presenter.
- **84% of 68 sampled frames** are above 45 dB on the same round-trip test; **63%** are above 50 dB. The 4K file is **5.2× larger** because YouTube gives 4K **5.8× the video bitrate** for 4× the pixels — not because it has more picture.
- **The person is real.** “El” is **Elena Markoska** — PhD in Engineering Science, University of Southern Denmark (2016–2019); Senior Data Scientist at the Bank of England; co-author of BoE Staff Working Papers 924 and 943. The 2020–21 “Walk the walk” channel is hers. The human is not fake. The **production** is synthetic.
- **The April 2026 timestamp is not the smoking gun.** `lmt` is YouTube's last-encode time, not the upload date. YouTube re-encodes old videos. The 4K upscale is proven by pixels, not metadata.

## Caveats

- One video. The 4K finding is specific to `rDKzAml5nn8`.
- AI-generated content is soft. A native 4K render of a smooth avatar could also round-trip well; the flat noise floor above 0.25 cyc/px and the 720≈1080 equality are what make “soft native 4K” unlikely.
- The native reference is a photo, not a video. It calibrates the codec/bitrate, not the content. The crowd b-roll is the ambiguous scene.
- PSNR is content-dependent. Conservative no-shift numbers are reported; subpixel alignment can add 1–2 dB.
- YouTube formats and `lmt` values are dated 26 October 2026.
